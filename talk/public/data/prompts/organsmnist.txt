You are an expert reader of medical images. You report only what is visible in the image, and you answer with the JSON object you are asked for and nothing else.

[user]
You are shown one image from a collection of 1-channel greyscale abdominal CT crops at a fixed abdominal window; each image is a 2D bounding-box crop around one organ taken from a CT volume in the sagittal plane. The image is 224 by 224 pixels, and comes with no clinical context, no patient information and no scale bar: judge only what this image shows.

Answer all 12 questions below about what is visible in this image. Each question lists its levels in order. For every question choose the one level that best fits this image.

1. body_side
   Judging from the surrounding structures caught in the crop (spine, body wall, midline vessels), does the main structure lie on the patient's right, near the midline, or on the patient's left? Remember that in a cross-sectional image the patient's left is shown on the right of the picture.
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     patient_right: the spine or the aorta sits toward the image-right of the crop, with the structure lying to the left of it on screen
     midline: body wall or paired muscles lie about equally far from the structure on both sides of the image, with no side landmark nearer than the other
     patient_left: the spine or the aorta sits toward the image-left of the crop, with the structure lying to the right of it on screen

2. craniocaudal_level
   From the surroundings visible in the crop, at what level of the trunk was this section taken: the pelvis, the lower abdomen, the upper abdomen, or the chest?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     pelvic: an iliac wing, pubic bone or rounded femoral head borders the crop, and no rib is present
     lower_abdominal: gas- or contrast-filled bowel loops and psoas muscle border the crop, with neither rib nor pelvic bone in view
     upper_abdominal: the lowest ribs border the crop alongside solid parenchymal organs, with no pelvic bone in view
     thoracic: ribs surround the crop and nearly black air-filled lung borders the structure

3. anteroposterior_position
   Do the surroundings place the main structure toward the back of the body (against the spine and the back muscles), in the middle, or toward the front body wall?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     posterior: a vertebral body and the thick paraspinal back muscles lie immediately against the structure
     central: neither the spine nor the front body wall touches the structure; both lie beyond it at the crop edge
     anterior: the front body wall, with its strap-like rectus muscle or the sternum, lies immediately against the structure

4. relative_size
   Compared with the landmarks caught at the edge of the crop (a vertebral body, a rib, the thickness of the body wall), is the main structure small, medium or large?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     small: a single landmark at the crop edge - one vertebra or one rib - appears magnified and fills much of that edge
     medium: two or three separate landmarks fit along the crop edge, each still plainly resolved
     large: many small landmarks crowd the crop edge at once - several ribs, vertebrae or bowel loops together

5. elongation
   Is the outline of the main structure roughly circular, moderately oval, or markedly elongated and strip-like?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     round: the two widths of the outline differ by less than about a quarter, and the shape fills most of the frame
     oval: the long width is roughly one and a half to three times the short one, leaving the frame corners empty
     elongated: the long axis runs more than about three times the short one, a strip that leaves most of the frame as background

6. contour_lobulation
   Is the outline smooth and even, mildly lobulated or wavy, or markedly irregular and feathery?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     smooth: the border runs as an unbroken curve, with no bump deeper than the thickness of the border line itself
     mildly_lobulated: one or two shallow bulges or gentle waves interrupt an otherwise continuous curve
     markedly_irregular: many bumps and clefts break the border, or it dissolves into a feathery fringe against the surrounding fat

7. hilar_notch
   Is there a single distinct indentation, notch or wedge cut into one side of the outline where vessels appear to enter?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: the border runs continuously round the structure with no indentation on any side
     subtle: one side is flattened or dimpled, but the dent is shallower than about a tenth of the structure's width
     prominent: a wedge deeper than about a tenth of the width is cut into one side, with vessels or fat running into it

8. relative_attenuation
   Overall, how bright is the main structure compared with the ordinary soft tissue and muscle visible around it?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     much_darker: nearly black at this window, clearly the darkest tissue in the crop and far below the muscle at the frame edge
     slightly_darker: a shade below the muscle at the frame edge, but plainly mid-grey rather than black
     similar: no step in grey level against the muscle at the frame edge; only the outline separates the two
     brighter: a clear step above the muscle at the frame edge, yet still well short of the white of bone
     much_brighter: among the brightest things in the crop, approaching the white of cortical bone or of dense contrast

9. interior_homogeneity
   Is the inside of the structure uniform in brightness, or does it contain clearly different regions, internal branching lines or pockets?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     homogeneous: one even grey fills the interior, broken by nothing more than a few thin vessel lines
     mildly_heterogeneous: faint mottling or a few branching lines cross the interior, but no region differs sharply from the rest
     markedly_heterogeneous: two or more distinct zones, one clearly brighter than the other, meet at sharp internal borders

10. bright_cortical_rim
   Is the structure outlined by a thin rim of the very brightest, calcium-density material, brighter than any soft tissue in the crop?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no calcium-white line follows the border anywhere; the edge is grey meeting grey
     partial: a calcium-white line brighter than any soft tissue follows part of the border and then breaks off
     complete: an unbroken calcium-white line rings the whole border, the brightest thing in the crop

11. boundary_definition
   How clearly is the edge of the main structure separated from what surrounds it: barely distinguishable, moderately defined, or crisp along most of its circumference?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     indistinct: the edge merges into the surrounding tissue over most of its length; you could not trace it confidently
     moderate: the edge is traceable over roughly half the circumference and blurred or interrupted over the rest
     sharp: a crisp step in brightness follows almost the whole circumference, often with a dark fat line beside it

12. adjacent_bone
   Are very bright, calcium-density structures such as vertebrae, ribs or pelvic bone visible at the edge of the crop, and if so how much of the edge do they occupy?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: nothing of calcium-white density appears anywhere along the crop edge
     small: one small patch of calcium-white touches the crop edge and occupies a minor part of it
     prominent: calcium-white runs along a long stretch of the crop edge, or several bony pieces border the crop at once
