[system]
You are an expert reader of medical images. You report only what is visible in the image, and you answer with the JSON object you are asked for and nothing else.

[user]
You are shown one image from a collection of haematoxylin-and-eosin-stained colorectal histology patches, 3-channel colour, 224 x 224 pixels at about 0.5 microns per pixel. The image is 224 by 224 pixels, and comes with no clinical context, no patient information and no scale bar: judge only what this image shows.

Answer all 12 questions below about what is visible in this image. Each question lists its levels in order. For every question choose the one level that best fits this image.

1. tissue_content
   How much of the field is filled with recognisable stained tissue structure rather than plain, featureless white space?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     none: no stained material anywhere: flat, even white or very pale grey across the whole field
     sparse: stained structure covers under about a quarter of the field; the pale rest holds no outlines
     moderate: a quarter to two thirds is stained, or empty spaces fill the field but each one is walled
     abundant: stained cells, fibres and nuclei fill the field, leaving no white gap wider than a nucleus

2. nuclear_density
   How densely packed are the cell nuclei, seen as small dark blue-purple bodies?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     none: not a single dark blue-purple nuclear body anywhere in the field
     few: fewer than about twenty nuclei, most lying more than several nuclear widths from the next
     moderate: nuclei roughly one to three nuclear widths apart, together covering well under a quarter of the field
     many: nuclei frequently touching, together covering roughly a third to a half of the field
     very_many: nuclei almost confluent, covering over half the field with little visible material between them

3. nuclear_pleomorphism
   Do the nuclei vary in size, shape and darkness, or are they all much alike?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     uniform: nuclei alike in size, shape and darkness, lying with their long axes similarly oriented
     mildly_variable: largest nuclei about twice the smallest; staining slightly uneven; most still share one orientation
     markedly_variable: largest nuclei three or more times the smallest, with coarse dark chromatin, prominent nucleoli and lost polarity

4. gland_formation
   Are there gland-like structures, that is rings or tubes of cells enclosing an open lumen?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no ring or tube of cells enclosing an open lumen anywhere in the field
     few: gland-like rings occupy less than half the field; the rest is solid sheets, cords or fibrous tissue
     many: gland-like rings occupy more than half the field

5. gland_regularity
   If gland-like structures are present, are they evenly sized, evenly spaced and smoothly outlined, or crowded, angulated and irregular in size and shape?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     no_glands: no ring or tube of cells encloses a lumen anywhere, so there is no gland outline to compare
     regular: glands alike in size, round or oval, evenly spaced, smoothly outlined, each with an open lumen
     mildly_irregular: glands differ up to about twofold in size, some branched or angulated, lumina still open and separate
     markedly_irregular: glands back-to-back or cribriform, sharply angulated, lumina slit-like or absent, sizes differing several-fold

6. goblet_cells
   Within any cell lining, are there clear rounded mucin droplets that push the nucleus to one side of the cell?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no clear rounded vacuole in the lining; every nucleus sits at the same level
     few: clear vacuoles in under about a quarter of the lining cells, scattered singly
     many: clear vacuoles in over half the lining cells, each pushing its nucleus against the cell base

7. fibre_alignment
   Where elongated fibre-like structures are present, do they run in tightly packed parallel bundles, or are they wavy, loose and haphazardly oriented?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     no_fibres: no elongated fibre-like structure anywhere; the field holds only round cells, granular material or empty space
     uniformly_parallel: almost every fibre runs on one axis, tightly packed, with no gaps or crossing bundles
     mixed: two or three fibre directions present, or hyalinised bands over a fifth of the field wide crossing looser fibres
     loose_and_haphazard: wavy fibres share no direction and are separated by pale amorphous ground substance

8. cytoplasm_eosinophilia
   Is the material between the nuclei mostly deep pink or red, or mostly pale, grey or blue-purple?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     pale_or_basophilic: material between nuclei is pale grey, blue or violet, with no saturated pink anywhere
     mixed: pink and pale blue-grey areas both present, neither covering more than about two thirds of the field
     strongly_eosinophilic: deep saturated pink fills over two thirds of the field between nuclei, with no blue-grey zones

9. extracellular_mucin
   Are there pools or wisps of pale blue-grey, structureless mucinous material lying outside cells?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no pale blue-grey structureless material lies outside cells anywhere in the field
     focal: pale blue-grey pools cover between about a tenth and half the field
     extensive: pale blue-grey structureless material covers more than half the field

10. fat_vacuoles
   Does the field show large empty round or polygonal spaces bounded by thin walls, in a honeycomb arrangement?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no large empty rounded space; any white area is unwalled or no wider than a nucleus
     few: one to three large empty rounded spaces, each many times wider than a nucleus
     many: empty walled spaces in a honeycomb cover over half the field, their walls thinner than a nucleus

11. small_round_cell_infiltrate
   Are there collections of small, round, uniformly dark nuclei with almost no visible cytoplasm around them?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no small dark round nuclei; nuclei present are elongated, pale or vesicular
     scattered: a mild, patchy increase of small dark round nuclei, singly or in small groups, not confluent
     dense: small dark round nuclei form a confluent band or sheet over about a third of the field

12. necrotic_debris
   Is there granular, fragmented material with broken nuclear dust and no intact cell outlines?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: every cell has an intact outline and a whole nucleus; no granular fragmented material
     focal: granular fragmented material in patches under about a third of the field, mostly inside gland lumina
     extensive: granular fragmented material with scattered nuclear dust covers over a third of the field
