[system]
You are an expert reader of medical images. You report only what is visible in the image, and you answer with the JSON object you are asked for and nothing else.

[user]
You are shown one image from a collection of 1-channel greyscale retinal optical coherence tomography (OCT) B-scans. The image is 224 by 224 pixels, and comes with no clinical context, no patient information and no scale bar: judge only what this image shows.

Answer all 9 questions below about what is visible in this image. Each question lists its levels in order. For every question choose the one level that best fits this image.

1. retinal_layer_integrity
   Are the banded retinal layers continuous and regularly stacked across the scan?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     intact: every band, down to the thin bright ellipsoid-zone line, runs unbroken from one edge of the frame to the other
     mildly_disrupted: one or two bands blur or break over less than about a quarter of the frame width, and the stacking order stays readable throughout
     markedly_disrupted: banding is lost over more than about a quarter of the frame width, or layers are so displaced that their order cannot be traced

2. foveal_contour
   Does the retinal surface at the centre form a smooth shallow depression?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     depressed: the inner surface dips into a smooth shallow concave pit whose floor lies below the inner surface on either side
     flattened: the central dip is shallow or gone, so the inner surface runs almost level across the middle of the frame
     elevated: the centre of the inner surface bulges upward and stands higher than the inner surface to either side of it

3. retinal_thickness
   How thick is the retina between its inner surface and the bright outer band?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     not_increased: the retina is at its thinnest in the centre of the frame and grows thicker towards both edges
     mildly_increased: the central retina is as thick as, or a little thicker than, the retina at the edges of the same frame
     markedly_increased: the central retina is more than about half again as thick as the retina at the edges of the same frame

4. intraretinal_cystoid_spaces
   Are there rounded dark low-reflectivity pockets inside the retinal layers?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: the layers between the inner surface and the bright outer band are evenly banded, with no dark rounded pocket among them
     few_small: one to three dark rounded pockets, each under about a third of the retinal thickness tall, with retinal tissue both above and below it
     many_or_large: four or more such pockets, or one taller than half the retinal thickness, each still roofed and floored by retinal tissue

5. subretinal_fluid
   Is there a dark empty space lifting the retina off the bright band beneath it?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: the outermost retinal bands sit directly on the bright outer band, with no dark gap between the two
     small: a shallow dark gap floored only by the bright outer band lifts the outermost retinal bands by under about a quarter of the retinal thickness
     large: a dome of dark space raises the whole outer retina more than a quarter of the retinal thickness above the bright outer band

6. rpe_line_contour
   Is the bright outer band smooth, gently domed in small bumps, or focally lifted?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     smooth: the bright outer band follows one even curve, and no bump on it is wider than about a hundredth of the frame width (63 um)
     small_domed_undulations: one or more smooth rounded domes lift the band, each under about a twentieth of the frame width, the band thin and unbroken over each
     large_irregular_elevation: a single lift wider than about a twentieth of the frame width, or one whose top is ragged, angular or multi-peaked rather than a smooth dome

7. subretinal_hyperreflective_material
   Is there bright solid-looking material between the retina and the bright outer band?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: the outermost retinal bands meet the bright outer band directly, with nothing bright and solid-looking interposed
     small: one bright patch lies between them, under about a third of the retinal thickness tall and narrower than a twentieth of the frame width
     extensive: bright material between them fills more than about a third of the retinal thickness, or runs wider than a twentieth of the frame width

8. hyperreflective_foci
   Are there small bright dots or specks scattered within the darker retinal layers?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: no discrete bright dot stands out from the darker layers lying above the bright outer band
     few: up to about ten separate bright dots, each no larger than the bright outer band is thick (30 um), scattered through the darker layers
     many: more than about ten such dots, often gathered into clusters or strung along one layer

9. shadowing_beneath_lesion
   Do bright structures cast vertical dark columns into the tissue beneath them?
   Levels, in order, and what each looks like:
     absent: tissue directly beneath every bright structure is as bright as the tissue beside it at the same depth
     mild: one faint dim column sits under a bright structure, no wider than that structure, and the layers it crosses can still be made out
     marked: dark columns under several bright structures blank out the layers beneath them all the way to the bottom of the frame

Reply with one JSON object and nothing else: exactly 9 keys, the question names above, each value one of that question's levels copied exactly as written.
